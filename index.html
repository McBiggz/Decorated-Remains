Ethical & Legal Sourcing

Animal remains are sourced through third-party vendors represented as ethical and legal sources. Decorated Remains does not knowingly purchase, use or sell protected or prohibited wildlife materials.

US Shipping

Shipping is limited to the United States at launch. Availability may still vary by species, material and destination law.

Final Sale

Due to the handmade, one-of-one nature of the work, all sales are final. Shipping damage should be documented immediately upon delivery.

Natural Variation

Bone and natural materials contain variation, age, texture and irregularities. These are features of the work, not manufacturing defects.
